X-Ray Corporation - S-199 Chemical Mixer

Product Overview

The IS-199 incorporates a unique combination of features that make it the mixer of choice for both users and technicians. Its Specific Gravity Mixing System allows for accurate automatic water addition whenever concentrated chemicals are introduced. All electronic components are chosen for dependability, and the straightforward circuit design eliminates the need for circuit boards, increasing reliability. A low level warning system alerts users when the solution is at or below 2.0 gallons, and resets automatically when mixing begins.

Features

  • Specific Gravity Mixing System – Ultimate convenience and accuracy
  • Rugged field-tested components
  • 24 Volt Operation – No circuit boards
  • Audible and visible low-level warning system
  • Easy-to-service design
  • UL/CSA approved external power supply

Technical Specifications

  • Voltage: 120 Volts AC
  • Dimensions:
    • Standard Model: 30"H x 24"W x 12"D
    • Space Saver: 21"H x 24"W x 12"D
  • Capacity:
    • Standard Model: 12.5 gallons (Developer & Fixer)
    • Space Saver: 7.5 gallons (Developer & Fixer)
  • Operating Range: 18–125 psi Water Pressure
