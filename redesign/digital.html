Source-Ray Direct-Digital Portable X-Ray Systems

Overview

The new Source-Ray DR Packages combine one of the great Source-Ray portable x-ray generator systems along with your choice of various flat-panel DR systems from top manufacturers creating a one-of-a-kind complete portable digital x-ray imaging system!

All systems feature:

  • Store-away laptop computer and tilt-out panel storage cabinet for easy storage and transportation.
  • Advanced imaging software with easy archive, print, and send tools.
  • Collimare™ Collimator with Laser Crosshairs.

SR-130 X-Ray Generator System Features

The SR-130 provides higher power, combined with state of the art technology, system reliability and low maintenance, all in a small footprint, lightweight package. The SR-130 provides hospital-quality imaging performed anywhere, anytime a portable X-Ray System is transported to a patient.

Key Features

  • kVp Range: 40 - 100 kVp, adjustable in 1kV increments with closed loop regulation.
  • mA Range: Adjustable 15 & 30 mA, closed loop regulation.
  • Total Max Power Output: 3kW.
  • Exposure Time: 0.01 – 4.00 seconds.
  • Exposure Switch: Detachable two-position.

PowerMax 1260 X-Ray Generator System Features

The PowerMax 1260 provides the maximum power obtainable from a line powered portable with state of the art technology, system reliability and low maintenance, all in a small footprint, lightweight package.

Key Features

  • kVp Range: 40 - 120 kVp, adjustable in 1kV increments with closed loop regulation.
  • mA Range: Adjustable 15 - 60 mA, with closed loop regulation.
  • Total Max Power Output: 3kW.
  • Exposure Time: 0.01 - 3.33 seconds.
  • Exposure Switch: Detachable two-position hand switch.
