X-Ray Corporation - SSOPTIMAX X-Ray Film Processor

Product Overview

The OPTIMAX is the world's premier 90-second automatic x-ray film processor. Lightweight yet rugged, it's ideal for both low and high-volume film processing. Manufactured in Germany by Protec Medical, the OPTIMAX meets international quality standards and delivers exceptional image quality while minimizing energy, water, and chemical use.

Features & Benefits

  • Lightweight, rugged tabletop design
  • 90-second processing from leading edge to leading edge
  • Energy and water-saving with smart run-time logic
  • Anti-oxidation and anti-crystallization for chemical efficiency
  • Smart replenishment sensor reduces chemical use
  • Front-panel temperature control
  • Top film feed and return
  • Spring-loaded rollers prevent jams
  • High-quality rollers for artifact-free images

Standard Equipment

  • All components for tabletop processing
  • Levelling legs
  • Spare parts kit
  • 6.6 gallon (25 L) replenishment tanks
  • Floating lid for developer tank
  • Water supply hose
  • All tubing and hardware
  • Manuals included
